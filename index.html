Questions

Do I need an Amazon Associates account?

Yes. Link Lister adds your own Associates tag to product links, so you need an approved Amazon Associates account and tag. Commissions are paid by Amazon to you, the same as any link you build by hand.

Where are my links stored?

In your browser's local storage on your computer. Link Lister has no servers holding your links. Use Save JSON or auto-backup to keep a copy, especially before reinstalling Chrome.

Which Amazon sites does it work on?

amazon.com product pages. Open a product, then click the Link Lister icon.

How do I get my Pro license key?

Right after checkout you're taken to a page showing your license key, with a copy button. Paste it into the extension and click Unlock. Keep the key: you'll need it if you reinstall.
