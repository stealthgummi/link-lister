Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Link Lister is a Chrome extension that builds Amazon affiliate links and keeps a list of the links you save. This page explains what data it uses.

What stays on your computer

  • Your Amazon Associates tag.
  • The links you save, with each product's title, ASIN and the date you saved it.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked.

All of this is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else. If you turn on auto-backup, Link Lister writes a backup file to a folder you choose on your own computer.

What the extension reads

When you click the Link Lister icon on an amazon.com product page, it reads that page's product title and ASIN to build your link. It doesn't read other sites, run in the background, or track your browsing.

What is sent over the internet

Only one thing: when you enter a Pro license key and click Unlock, the key is sent to our license server to confirm the purchase. The server checks the key with our payment processor, Stripe, and replies whether it's valid. Nothing else about you or your links is sent.

Payments

Pro purchases are handled by Stripe. We receive the details Stripe provides for a purchase (such as your email and the amount paid) so we can support your order. We never see or store your card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use analytics or ads inside the extension.
  • We don't change links you didn't ask Link Lister to build.

Deleting your data

Use Clear All in the Link Lister dashboard, or remove the extension. Both delete everything it stored in your browser. Backup files you saved are yours to keep or delete.
